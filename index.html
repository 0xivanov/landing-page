04 / FAQ

A few useful
answers.

What is Launchstead? +

Launchstead is a hosting workspace for independent builders and small teams. It is being developed around a simple flow: upload your code, create a release, and share the result.

What can I deploy? +

We are developing and testing support for static HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites and Node.js applications. Preview access is arranged directly.

How will hosting be priced? +

We plan to offer recurring hosting subscriptions. Public pricing is not available yet, and this site does not collect payments. Contact us to discuss a preview and your requirements.

Is it publicly available? +

Not yet. Launchstead is currently in a limited private preview. Reach out by email to discuss access or tell us what you are building.

Will there be custom domains or commerce? +

Both are planned for a future stage. They are coming next, and are not available in the current preview.
