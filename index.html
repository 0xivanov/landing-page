04 / FAQ

A few useful
answers.

What is Launchstead? +

Launchstead is a hosting workspace for freelancers and small agencies shipping client websites. It is being developed around a simple flow: upload your code, create a release, and share the result.

What can I deploy? +

You can upload static HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites or supported Node.js applications. Node.js builds run on ARM64 workers; discuss native dependencies, databases and persistent storage needs with us first. Access is arranged directly.

Is it ready for production? +

Launchstead is in limited private early access. There is no public signup, paid hosting plan or service-level commitment yet, so discuss your project with us before relying on it for a critical client launch.

How will hosting be priced? +

We plan to offer recurring hosting subscriptions. Public pricing is not available yet, and this site does not collect payments. Contact us to discuss a preview and your requirements.

Can I buy a domain or take payments here? +

Not yet. You can connect a domain you already own. Domain purchasing and real customer payments are not available yet.
