Privacy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy covers the public Launchstead website and enquiries sent to its operator.

A simple website

This site does not include advertising trackers, third-party analytics, a mailing-list signup or a contact form. It does not set application cookies or use browser storage to track visitors.

Hosting and technical information

Our hosting and network providers may process information such as your IP address, requested page, browser information and request time to deliver the website and maintain security. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. You can read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details about its processing.

When you contact us

Email links open your own email application. If you send an enquiry, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and discuss your project. Please do not send passwords, API keys or payment-card details.

Keeping and removing information

Enquiry information is kept as needed to respond and maintain the related correspondence. You can ask about the information you have shared, request corrections or request deletion by emailing ivanivanov.ii726@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.

Future hosting accounts

Public customer signup and live payments are not enabled on this marketing website. Any future customer portal will explain its account, hosting and payment data practices before you sign up.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Launchstead operator at ivanivanov.ii726@gmail.com.
